# Cast and assets — every @tag in Capyverse Odyssey

Originals are in `media/library/<type>s/<Tag>.<ext>`; URLs in `media/library/library.json`.
In Shot Forge, typing `@Tag` attaches that asset's reference image automatically.
Tags are case-insensitive in practice (Gus writes `@capy_gp` for `Capy_gp`).

## The playable squad (use these most)

Every hero wears a **red / yellow / blue harness** with **small jetpack thrusters on the left and
right of the belt/waist** (not a backpack), plus a back-mounted signature weapon.

| Hero | Tags | Look | Signature prop / VFX |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cappy** — protagonist | `@capy_gp` (turnaround sheet), `@Cappy_flying` | Chunky brown capybara, big round amber eye | **Popcorn gatling**: red-and-white striped popcorn bucket with a multi-barrel striped cannon. Gus calls it "the weapon barrels above the large orange capybara". *Note: the `@Cappy_flying` sheet shows a disco-ball cannon instead; in the current game render Cappy has the popcorn gatling.* |
| **Moody** | `@moody_gp` (sheet), `@Moody_flying`, `@moody_Nobelt` (no harness) | Small pink/mauve pygmy hippo | **Disco-ball cannon** (yellow barrel + mirrored disco ball). VFX: light flares from the ball's facets. |
| **Axie** | `@axie_gp` (sheet), `@Axie_flying`, `axie_nobelt` (filed under environments) | Lilac/purple axolotl, frilly gills, grumpy brows | **Wooden ballista** with red bolts. |
| **Jim** | `@jim_gp` (sheet), `@Jim_flying`, `@Jim` (no harness) | Gray-and-white chunky bulldog, dark tech harness | **Trumpet**. VFX: confetti bursting from the bell. |
